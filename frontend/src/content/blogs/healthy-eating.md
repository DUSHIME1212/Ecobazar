Eating a healthy, balanced diet is one of the most important things you can do to protect your health. In fact, up to 80% of premature heart disease and stroke can be prevented through your life choices and habits, such as eating a healthy diet and being physically active.

### Key Components of a Healthy Diet

A healthy diet includes a variety of foods from different food groups. It's not about strict limitations, but about feeling great, having more energy, and improving your health.

1. **Eat Plenty of Vegetables and Fruits**: They are packed with vitamins, minerals, and fiber.
2. **Whole Grains**: Choose whole grain bread, pasta, and rice over refined versions.
3. **Protein**: Include a variety of protein sources, such as beans, nuts, seeds, and lean meats.
