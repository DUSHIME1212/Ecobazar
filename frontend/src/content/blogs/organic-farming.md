Organic food is food produced by methods that comply with the standards of organic farming. Standards vary worldwide, but organic farming features practices that cycle resources, promote ecological balance, and conserve biodiversity.

### The Benefits of Organic Farming

Organic farming is not just about avoiding pesticides; it's a holistic approach to agriculture that focuses on the health of the soil and the ecosystem. By using natural fertilizers and crop rotation, organic farmers maintain the fertility of the land for future generations.

- **No Synthetic Pesticides**: Organic farming avoids the use of harmful chemicals that can contaminate our water and soil.
- **Better Taste**: Many people find that organic produce has a more intense and natural flavor.
- **Supports Biodiversity**: Organic farms provide a safe haven for bees, butterflies, and other essential pollinators.
